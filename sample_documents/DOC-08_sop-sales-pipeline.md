# SOP – Sales Pipeline Management — FourAngryBirds EdTech & HR Solutions JSC

**Version 1.0 — Effective 2026-01-01**

## 1. Purpose
Defines how the Sales function manages leads from first contact through closed deal.

## 2. Pipeline Stages Definition
Lead → Qualified → Proposal Sent → Negotiation → Closed-Won / Closed-Lost.

## 3. Lead Qualification Criteria [ROLE-SPECIFIC: Sales Executive]
A lead is "Qualified" only when the Sales Executive has confirmed: budget authority, timeline within 6 months, and a genuine training/HR-tech need. Unqualified leads must be marked Closed-Lost with a reason code.

## 4. CRM Data Entry Standards [MANDATORY]
Every customer interaction must be logged in the CRM within 24 hours. Customer contact data entered into the CRM is subject to Data Privacy Policy (**DOC-05**) consent requirements.

## 5. Deal Approval Thresholds [Conditional]
Deals under 500,000,000 VND may be closed directly by the Sales Executive. Deals of 500,000,000 VND or more require Branch Manager approval before the proposal is sent.

## 6. Reporting Cadence
Sales Executive submits a weekly pipeline summary; Branch Manager reviews monthly forecast accuracy.

## 7. Optional Guidance [OPTIONAL]
Sales Executives are encouraged to use the internal upsell playbook for renewal conversations, though it is not mandatory for deal closure.
