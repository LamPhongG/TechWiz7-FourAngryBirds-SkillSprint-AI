# SOP – Financial Reimbursement — FourAngryBirds EdTech & HR Solutions JSC

**Version 1.0 — Effective 2026-01-01**

## 1. Purpose & Scope
Defines the process for employees to submit and be reimbursed for approved business expenses.

## 2. Eligible Expense Categories
Travel, client entertainment (within policy limits), training materials, and approved software subscriptions.

## 3. Submission Process & Required Documentation [MANDATORY]
Employees must submit original receipts and a completed expense form within **15 calendar days** of the expense being incurred. Claims submitted after 30 days are automatically rejected.

## 4. Approval Matrix [ROLE-SPECIFIC]
- **Finance Associate**: processes and verifies all claims for policy compliance.
- Claims under 5,000,000 VND: approved by the employee's direct manager.
- Claims of 5,000,000 VND or more: require **Branch Manager** approval in addition to the direct manager.

## 5. Reimbursement Timeline
Approved claims are paid within **10 business days** of Finance Associate verification.

## 6. Rejected Claims & Appeal Process
Rejected claims include a written reason. Employees may appeal once, in writing, to the Finance Associate's manager within 5 business days.

## 7. Compliance Note [Cross-Reference]
Receipt images may contain personal payment data and must be stored per Data Privacy Policy (**DOC-05 §5**) retention rules — not indefinitely.
