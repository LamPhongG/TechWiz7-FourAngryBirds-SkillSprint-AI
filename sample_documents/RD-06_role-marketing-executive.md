# Role Description — Marketing Executive
**FourAngryBirds EdTech & HR Solutions JSC — Marketing Department**

## 1. Role Summary
The Marketing Executive runs digital campaigns and manages lead/contact data collected through marketing channels, reporting to **Branch Manager**.

## 2. Core Responsibilities
- Run campaigns in compliance with data-privacy and third-party sharing rules.
- Secure marketing/analytics tooling with strong authentication.
- Collect and process campaign lead data only with recorded consent.
- Maintain professional conduct in all external-facing communications.

## 3. Required Competencies
Professional conduct in external communications, marketing-systems access security, acceptable-use compliance, data-privacy-aware campaign management, third-party data-sharing compliance.

## 4. Governing Documents
- Workplace Conduct Policy (**DOC-04**, §2).
- Information Security Policy (**DOC-06**, §2 password/MFA, §3 acceptable use).
- Data Privacy Policy (**DOC-05**, §3 consent, §6 third-party sharing).
- Employee Handbook (**DOC-01**) and HR Leave Policy (**DOC-03**).

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions
**Q: Can I upload our lead list directly to an ad platform's audience-matching tool?**
A: Only under a signed data-processing agreement with Data Privacy Officer sign-off (R045) — never as an ad-hoc upload.

**Q: Do I need MFA if I only manage social media, not customer data directly?**
A: R042 applies to any system handling customer data; check with IT Security whether your specific tools are in scope before assuming an exemption.

## 7. Escalation & Reporting Line
Marketing Executive → **Branch Manager** (campaign approvals, third-party data-sharing sign-off routed via HR Executive as Data Privacy Officer).
