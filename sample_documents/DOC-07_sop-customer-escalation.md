# SOP – Customer Escalation Process — FourAngryBirds EdTech & HR Solutions JSC

**Version 1.0 — Effective 2026-01-01**

## 1. Purpose
This SOP defines how customer complaints and support issues are escalated through the Customer Support function.

## 2. Scope
Applies primarily to the Customer Support Executive role, with Tier 2/3 involvement from Team Leader/Tech Lead and Branch Manager.

## 3. Escalation Tiers Overview
- Tier 1 — first response, owned by Customer Support Executive.
- Tier 2 — formal escalation, owned jointly by Customer Support Executive and Team Leader.
- Tier 3 — management involvement, owned by Branch Manager.

## 4. Escalation Procedure

### 4.1 Tier 1 – Initial Response
Customer Support Executive must acknowledge any incoming customer issue within **2 business hours** and attempt first-contact resolution using the approved knowledge base.

### 4.2 Tier 2 – Formal Escalation Trigger and Authority Levels [MANDATORY]
**Requirement ID R001.** Any unresolved customer complaint that exceeds **24 hours** without resolution, or that involves a safety, legal, or financial risk, **must** be escalated by the Customer Support Executive to a Team Leader within **1 business hour** of that threshold being identified. Every Customer Support Executive is mandatorily required to complete Tier 2 Escalation Authority training during **Week 1** of onboarding, before handling escalations unsupervised. Priority: **High**.

### 4.3 Tier 3 – Management Involvement
If a Tier 2 escalation remains unresolved after 48 hours, the Team Leader must escalate to the Branch Manager, who has final authority to approve compensation, refunds, or service-level exceptions.

## 5. Documentation Requirements
Every escalation must be logged with: ticket ID, customer ID, tier reached, resolution, and time-to-resolution. Logs are subject to audit per Section 6.
