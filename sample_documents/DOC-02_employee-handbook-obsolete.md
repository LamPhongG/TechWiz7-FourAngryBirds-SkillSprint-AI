# Employee Handbook — FourAngryBirds EdTech & HR Solutions JSC

**Version 1.0 — Effective 2023-01-01 (superseded)**

## 1. Introduction & Purpose
This handbook sets out the baseline policies, expectations, and benefits applicable to every employee of FourAngryBirds EdTech & HR Solutions JSC ("the Company").

## 2. Company Overview
FourAngryBirds EdTech & HR Solutions JSC is a ~180-person EdTech and HR-solutions company headquartered in Ho Chi Minh City. (Note: the Đà Nẵng office had not yet opened at the time this version was effective.)

## 3. Employment Basics
### 3.1 Working Hours
Standard working hours are 08:30–17:30, Monday to Friday.

### 3.2 Probation Period
All new hires undergo a 45-day probation period.

## 4. Code of Conduct
All employees must comply with the Workplace Conduct Policy (DOC-04).

## 5. Compensation & Benefits Overview
### 5.1 Leave Entitlement
Base annual leave entitlement is defined in the HR Leave Policy (DOC-03).

## 6. Leave Carryover Rule [SUPERSEDED]
Unused annual leave days **do not carry over** to the following calendar year and are forfeited on 31 December. **This rule has been replaced** by DOC-01 v2.0 §6, which permits carrying over up to 5 days until 31 March of the following year. This document is retained only as a superseded-policy test case for the contradiction-detection pipeline.

## 7. IT & Data Handling Basics
Employees handling customer data must follow basic confidentiality guidelines; no formal Data Privacy Policy existed at the time this version was effective.

## 8. Acknowledgement
Every employee must acknowledge receipt of this handbook within 5 business days of their joining date.
