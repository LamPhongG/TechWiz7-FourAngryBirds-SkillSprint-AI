# Employee Handbook — FourAngryBirds EdTech & HR Solutions JSC

**Version 2.0 — Effective 2026-01-01**

## 1. Introduction & Purpose
This handbook sets out the baseline policies, expectations, and benefits applicable to every employee of FourAngryBirds EdTech & HR Solutions JSC ("the Company"). It applies company-wide unless a role-specific or department-specific document states otherwise (see Section 8, Cross-References).

## 2. Company Overview
FourAngryBirds EdTech & HR Solutions JSC is a ~250-person EdTech and HR-solutions company headquartered in Ho Chi Minh City, with a secondary office in Đà Nẵng opened in 2025. The Company operates across Sales, Customer Support, Human Resources, Finance, Operations, Marketing, Engineering, Branch Management, and Data functions.

## 3. Employment Basics
### 3.1 Working Hours
Standard working hours are 08:30–17:30, Monday to Friday, with core collaboration hours of 09:00–16:00. Engineering and Data roles may adopt flexible start times with manager approval.

### 3.2 Probation Period
All new hires undergo a 60-day probation period, during which onboarding modules assigned by the SkillSprint AI platform must be completed (see DOC-11, SOP – Employee Onboarding Process, added in Phase 2).

## 4. Code of Conduct
All employees must comply with the Workplace Conduct Policy (**DOC-04**) in full. This handbook does not restate conduct rules; DOC-04 is the authoritative source.

## 5. Compensation & Benefits Overview
### 5.1 Leave Entitlement
Base annual leave entitlement and role-specific variations are defined in the HR Leave Policy (**DOC-03**). This handbook governs only the carryover rule (Section 6 below), which takes precedence over any earlier version.

### 5.2 Expense Reimbursement
Expense claims follow the SOP – Financial Reimbursement (**DOC-09**).

## 6. Leave Carryover Rule [MANDATORY — ALL ROLES]
Effective with this version (v2.0), employees may carry over a **maximum of 5 unused annual leave days** into the following calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by **31 March** of the following year or they are forfeited. This rule **replaces** the no-carryover rule stated in the obsolete Employee Handbook v1.0 (**DOC-02**, superseded 2026-01-01).

## 7. IT & Data Handling Basics
Every employee must complete Information Security Policy (**DOC-06**) and Data Privacy Policy (**DOC-05**) training within the first 30 days of employment. Roles handling customer or personal data (Data Analyst, HR Executive, Finance Associate) are subject to additional access controls defined in DOC-05 §4.

Every employee must digitally acknowledge receipt and understanding of this handbook within 5 business days of their joining date. Acknowledgement status is tracked on the Employee Learning Dashboard.
