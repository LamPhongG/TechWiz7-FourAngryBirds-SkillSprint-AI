# Role Description — Finance Associate
**FourAngryBirds EdTech & HR Solutions JSC — Finance Department**

## 1. Role Summary
The Finance Associate processes employee expense reimbursements and safeguards financial and payment data. Reports to **Branch Manager**.

## 2. Core Responsibilities
- Verify receipts and documentation before approving reimbursement claims.
- Apply the approval matrix correctly, routing large claims to Branch Manager.
- Pay approved claims within the defined SLA.
- Retain and dispose of financial records (including receipt images) per the retention schedule.
- Secure financial systems with strong authentication.

## 3. Required Competencies
Expense-claim verification, reimbursement approval-threshold judgment, reimbursement SLA management, financial-record data retention, least-privilege data access (Finance), financial-systems access security.

## 4. Governing Documents
- SOP – Financial Reimbursement (**DOC-09**) — primary reference.
- Data Privacy Policy (**DOC-05**, §4 access roles).
- Information Security Policy (**DOC-06**, §2 password/MFA).
- Employee Handbook (**DOC-01**) and HR Leave Policy (**DOC-03**).

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions
**Q: A claim is 4,800,000 VND — does it still need Branch Manager approval?**
A: No — only claims at or above 5,000,000 VND require Branch Manager sign-off in addition to the direct manager (R026).

**Q: Can I keep receipt images indefinitely for audit purposes?**
A: No — R028 requires disposal per the Data Privacy Policy retention schedule (DOC-05 §5), not indefinite storage.

**Q: Is MFA optional if I only process small claims?**
A: No — R030 requires MFA on all systems handling financial data, regardless of claim size.

## 7. Escalation & Reporting Line
Finance Associate → **Branch Manager** (claims ≥ 5,000,000 VND, rejected-claim appeals).
