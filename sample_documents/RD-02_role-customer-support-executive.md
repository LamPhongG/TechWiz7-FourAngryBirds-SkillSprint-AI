# Role Description — Customer Support Executive
**FourAngryBirds EdTech & HR Solutions JSC — Customer Support Department**

## 1. Role Summary
The Customer Support Executive is the first point of contact for customer issues, owns Tier 1 resolution, and escalates through the defined tier structure. Reports to **Team Leader/Tech Lead**.

## 2. Core Responsibilities
- Acknowledge and attempt first-contact resolution on incoming issues within SLA.
- Escalate unresolved or high-risk complaints to Tier 2 within the defined trigger window.
- Log every escalation with full traceability (ticket, customer, tier, resolution, time).
- Handle customer data only through approved, logged tooling.
- Maintain professional, customer-facing conduct across all channels.

## 3. Required Competencies
Escalation judgment & Tier-2 authority awareness, first-response SLA management, escalation documentation discipline, customer-facing professional conduct, secure customer-data handling.

## 4. Governing Documents

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions
**Q: A customer complaint has been open for 20 hours with no safety/legal/financial risk — must I escalate now?**
A: Not yet. The mandatory escalation trigger (R001) is 24 hours, or immediately if safety/legal/financial risk is involved — whichever comes first.

**Q: Can I export customer data to a spreadsheet to investigate an issue faster?**
A: No — R005 explicitly requires approved, logged tooling only; direct database/export access is prohibited even for legitimate investigation.

**Q: Who do I escalate a Tier 2 case to?**
A: Your Team Leader (see DOC-07 §4.2 and Role Description RD-10, Team Leader/Tech Lead, R073).

## 7. Escalation & Reporting Line
Customer Support Executive → **Team Leader/Tech Lead** (Tier 2, within 1 business hour of the 24-hour threshold) → **Branch Manager** (Tier 3, if unresolved after 48 hours).
