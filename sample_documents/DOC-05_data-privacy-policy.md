# Data Privacy Policy — FourAngryBirds EdTech & HR Solutions JSC

**Version 1.0 — Effective 2026-01-01**

## 1. Purpose & Regulatory Basis
This policy governs the handling of personal data belonging to employees, customers, and partners, in line with Vietnam's Decree 13/2023 on Personal Data Protection.

## 2. Scope of Personal Data Covered
Includes, but is not limited to: employee records, customer contact details, payment information, and learner progress data processed by the SkillSprint AI platform.

## 3. Data Collection & Consent [MANDATORY]
Personal data may only be collected for a stated business purpose, with the data subject's informed consent recorded at the point of collection.

## 4. Data Access Roles & Least-Privilege [ROLE-SPECIFIC]
- **Data Analyst**: read access to anonymized/aggregated datasets only, by default.
- **HR Executive**: access to employee personal records, restricted to HR-necessary fields.
- **Finance Associate**: access to payment and reimbursement data only.
Access beyond these defaults requires written approval from the Data Privacy Officer (a designated HR Executive responsibility).

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion Schedule
Employee records are retained for 5 years after termination of employment, per statutory requirement. Customer data is retained for 3 years after the last active engagement, then anonymized or deleted.

## 6. Third-Party Data Sharing Restrictions [MANDATORY]
Personal data must not be shared with any third party without a signed data-processing agreement and Data Privacy Officer sign-off.

## 7. Breach Notification Procedure
Any suspected data breach must be reported within 24 hours through the incident channel defined in the Information Security Policy (**DOC-06 §5**). The Data Privacy Officer must notify affected individuals within statutory deadlines where required.
