# Role Description — Sales Executive
**FourAngryBirds EdTech & HR Solutions JSC — Sales Department**

## 1. Role Summary
The Sales Executive owns the B2B sales pipeline for EdTech/HR-solutions accounts, from first contact through closed deal, and reports to the **Branch Manager**.

## 2. Core Responsibilities
- Qualify inbound and outbound leads against budget, timeline, and need criteria.
- Maintain accurate, timely CRM records for every customer interaction.
- Prepare proposals and route large deals for the required approval before sending.
- Support renewal and upsell conversations using approved playbooks.
- Represent the Company professionally in client meetings and site visits.

## 3. Required Competencies
Lead qualification judgment, CRM data hygiene, deal-approval threshold awareness, client-facing presentation standard, data-privacy-aware prospecting, upsell technique.

- Workplace Conduct Policy (**DOC-04**, dress code §4.2)

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions
**Q: Can I close a 400,000,000 VND deal without Branch Manager approval?**
A: Yes — only deals at or above 500,000,000 VND require Branch Manager sign-off (DOC-08 §5, R011).

**Q: Do I need to log a call that didn't result in a qualified lead?**
A: Yes — every customer interaction goes into the CRM within 24 hours (DOC-08 §4, R010), regardless of outcome.

**Q: Is the upsell playbook mandatory?**
A: No, it's a recommended reference (R012, Optional) — useful for renewal conversations but not required for deal closure.

## 7. Escalation & Reporting Line
Sales Executive → **Branch Manager** (deal approvals ≥ 500,000,000 VND, disciplinary matters, performance review).
