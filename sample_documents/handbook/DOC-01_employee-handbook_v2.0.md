# FourAngryBirds Employee Handbook (v2.0)

## 1. Welcome to FourAngryBirds
Welcome to FourAngryBirds EdTech & HR Solutions. This handbook outlines company policies, culture, and operational standards.

## 2. Employment Categories & Probation
All new hires undergo a standard probation period of 60 calendar days unless specified otherwise in the employment agreement. Performance evaluations are conducted prior to day 55.

## 3. Working Hours & Attendance
Standard office hours are Monday through Friday, 08:30 to 17:30 with a 1-hour lunch break. Core collaboration hours are 09:00 to 16:00.

## 4. Code of Conduct Overview
All team members must act with integrity, respect colleagues and clients, and uphold high professional standards across all communication channels.

## 5. Compensation & Benefits
Salaries are paid monthly on the 25th. Benefits include premium healthcare, annual performance bonuses, and continuous learning allowances.

## 6. Annual Leave & Carryover Rules
Employees accrue 12 days of annual leave per year. In this version 2.0, annual leave cannot be carried over into the following calendar year and must be taken within the calendar year accrued.

## 7. Workplace Health & Safety
We are committed to maintaining a safe, healthy workplace. Report any physical or psychological workplace hazards to HR immediately.

## 8. Company Equipment & IT Assets
Laptops and access credentials are provided solely for authorized business use and remain the property of FourAngryBirds.

## 9. Policy Acknowledgement [MANDATORY]
All newly joined employees must digitally acknowledge receipt and understanding of the Employee Handbook within 5 business days of joining through the internal portal.
