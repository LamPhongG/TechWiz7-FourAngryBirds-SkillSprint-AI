# HR Leave Policy — FourAngryBirds EdTech & HR Solutions JSC

**Version 1.0 — Effective 2026-01-01**

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This policy defines annual leave, sick leave, and parental leave entitlements for all employees. It works together with the Employee Handbook (**DOC-01**), which governs carryover rules.

## 2. Annual Leave Entitlement [MANDATORY — ALL ROLES]
Standard entitlement is **12 working days per year**, accrued monthly (1 day/month).

### 2.1 Role-Specific Variation [ROLE-SPECIFIC]
- **Team Leader / Tech Lead**: 15 days/year (seniority allowance).
- **Branch Manager**: 15 days/year (seniority allowance).
- All other roles: standard 12 days/year.

## 3. Sick Leave [MANDATORY]
Employees are entitled to paid sick leave per Vietnamese Labor Code provisions. A medical certificate is **required** for any sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive working days and must be submitted to HR within 3 business days of return.

## 4. Maternity / Paternity Leave [MANDATORY — Compliance]
- Maternity leave: 6 months, per Social Insurance Law.
- Paternity leave: 5–14 working days depending on birth circumstances.
HR Executive is responsible for verifying eligibility and filing social-insurance paperwork within statutory deadlines.

## 5. Unpaid Leave Request [OPTIONAL / Conditional]
Employees may request unpaid leave beyond their entitlement, subject to manager and HR Executive approval, on a case-by-case basis. Not applicable to probationary employees (first 60 days, see DOC-01 §3.2).

## 6. Leave Approval Workflow
1. Employee submits request via the HR portal.
2. Direct manager approves or rejects within 2 business days.
3. HR Executive records the approved leave and updates entitlement balance.

## 7. Carryover & Forfeiture
Carryover of unused annual leave is governed by the **current Employee Handbook (DOC-01 §6)**. Do not rely on any prior handbook version for this rule — DOC-02 is obsolete and its leave-carryover clause has been superseded.
