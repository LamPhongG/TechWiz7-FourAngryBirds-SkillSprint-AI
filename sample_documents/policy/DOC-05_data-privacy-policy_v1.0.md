# Data Privacy & Protection Policy

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This policy governs how personal data belonging to clients, candidates, employees, and learners is processed in compliance with Decree 13/2023/ND-CP.

## 2. Data Classification
Personal data is classified into Basic Personal Data and Sensitive Personal Data. All processing requires legal justification and adequate safeguards.

## 3. Customer & Prospect Data Consent [MANDATORY]
Sales, marketing, and support staff must obtain informed, recorded consent before collecting or entering customer contact data into the CRM. Data must only be used for the stated business purpose.

## 4. Employee Records Access Boundaries [MANDATORY]
Access to employee personal records is strictly restricted to HR-necessary fields only under the principle of least privilege. Personnel outside authorized HR roles may not inspect employee personal files.

## 5. Data Subject Rights
Individuals have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of their personal information by submitting a formal request to privacy@fourangrybirds.vn.

## 6. Incident Reporting & Breach Protocol
Any suspected data breach or unauthorized disclosure must be reported to the Data Privacy Officer within 1 hour and notified to authorities within 72 hours.
