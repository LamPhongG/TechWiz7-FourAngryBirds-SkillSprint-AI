# Sales Team Playbook (unofficial)
**Shared internally by the Sales team, not issued by HR or Operations**

## Closing Deals Faster
To speed up our sales cycle, Sales Executives are authorized to close deals of up to
**700,000,000 VND** on their own judgment, without needing Branch Manager sign-off. Only
escalate to the Branch Manager for deals above that amount.
