WORKPLACE CONDUCT POLICY — FOURANGRYBIRDS EDTECH & HR SOLUTIONS JSC
===================================================================

Version 1.0 — Effective 2026-01-01

1. Purpose
----------

This policy defines the standards of professional behavior expected from every employee,
regardless of role or department.

2. Professional Behavior Standards [MANDATORY — ALL ROLES]
----------------------------------------------------------

Employees must treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect, communicate
professionally in all channels, and avoid conduct that could damage the Company's
reputation.

3. Anti-Harassment & Anti-Discrimination [MANDATORY — Compliance]
-----------------------------------------------------------------

The Company prohibits harassment or discrimination based on gender, age, religion,
disability, or any other protected characteristic. Violations must be reported to HR
Executive immediately and are handled under Section 6 (Disciplinary Process).

4. Dress Code
-------------

4.1 General Standard

Business-casual attire is expected in all offices.

4.2 Client-Facing Roles [ROLE-SPECIFIC: Sales Executive, Branch Manager]

Sales Executive and Branch Manager roles must wear business-formal attire during client
meetings and site visits.

5. Conflict of Interest Disclosure [MANDATORY]
----------------------------------------------

Employees must disclose any outside business interest, family relationship with a vendor,
or other potential conflict of interest to HR Executive within 10 business days of it
arising.

6. Disciplinary Process
-----------------------

Violations are handled in three stages: verbal warning, written warning, termination —
proportional to severity. Security-related violations (e.g., data mishandling) escalate
directly per Information Security Policy (DOC-06 §5), bypassing the verbal-warning stage.

7. Exceptions [EXCEPTION]
-------------------------

Operations Coordinator staff conducting field visits to partner schools are exempt from
the office dress code (Section 4.1) while on-site, but must still comply with Section 2
(Professional Behavior Standards).

8. Cross-References
-------------------
