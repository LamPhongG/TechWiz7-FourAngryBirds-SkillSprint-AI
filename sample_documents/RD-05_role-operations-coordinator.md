# Role Description — Operations Coordinator
**FourAngryBirds EdTech & HR Solutions JSC — Operations Department**

## 1. Role Summary
The Operations Coordinator supports day-to-day branch and field operations, including visits to partner schools, and reports to **Branch Manager**.

## 2. Core Responsibilities
- Coordinate field visits to partner schools, following the office/field conduct rules.
- Use only company-approved systems and secure remote/VPN access during field work.
- Collect partner-school data only with a stated purpose and recorded consent.
- Maintain professional conduct both in-office and during field engagements.

## 3. Required Competencies
Field-vs-office conduct judgment, field-professional conduct, acceptable-use compliance, remote/field access security, data-privacy-aware field data collection.

## 4. Governing Documents
- Workplace Conduct Policy (**DOC-04**, §2 conduct, §7 field-visit dress-code exception).
- Information Security Policy (**DOC-06**, §3 acceptable use, §6 remote access).
- Data Privacy Policy (**DOC-05**, §3 consent).
- Employee Handbook (**DOC-01**) and HR Leave Policy (**DOC-03**).

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions
**Q: Am I exempt from the office dress code during a partner-school visit?**
A: Yes (R033) — but Section 2's professional-conduct standard (R034) still applies at all times, on-site or off.

**Q: Can I use my personal laptop to access partner-school records while in the field?**
A: No — R036 requires an approved, encrypted device over company VPN for remote/field access.

## 7. Escalation & Reporting Line
Operations Coordinator → **Branch Manager** (day-to-day operations, incident reporting per DOC-06 §5).
