# Role Description — HR Executive
**FourAngryBirds EdTech & HR Solutions JSC — Human Resources Department**

## 1. Role Summary
The HR Executive administers leave, conduct, and data-privacy processes for the branch, and acts as the designated **Data Privacy Officer** function described in the Data Privacy Policy. Reports to **Branch Manager**.

## 2. Core Responsibilities
- Verify and file statutory leave paperwork (maternity/paternity) within legal deadlines.
- Record approved leave and maintain accurate entitlement balances.
- Intake and handle anti-harassment/anti-discrimination reports and conflict-of-interest disclosures.
- Act as Data Privacy Officer: enforce least-privilege access to employee records and lead breach notification.

## 3. Required Competencies
Statutory leave compliance filing, leave-workflow administration, harassment-complaint intake & handling, conflict-of-interest intake, least-privilege data access (HR), breach-notification response.

## 4. Governing Documents
- HR Leave Policy (**DOC-03**) — primary reference for leave administration.
- Workplace Conduct Policy (**DOC-04**, §3 harassment, §5 conflict of interest).
- Data Privacy Policy (**DOC-05**, §4 access roles, §7 breach notification).
- Employee Handbook (**DOC-01**).

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions
**Q: Can I access an employee's full personnel file for a routine leave request?**
A: No — R021 restricts access to HR-necessary fields only; full-file access requires a specific, justified purpose.

**Q: How quickly must I respond to a harassment complaint escalated from a manager?**
A: Immediately upon escalation (R019) — this is one of the highest-priority requirements for this role.

**Q: What happens if a data breach is confirmed after hours?**
A: The Data Privacy Officer function (HR Executive) must still notify affected individuals within the statutory deadline from DOC-05 §7 — there is no after-hours exemption.

## 7. Escalation & Reporting Line
HR Executive → **Branch Manager** (branch-level HR matters, disciplinary escalation per DOC-04 §6).
