# Information Security Policy — FourAngryBirds EdTech & HR Solutions JSC

**Version 1.0 — Effective 2026-01-01**

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This policy defines minimum security requirements for accessing company systems and data, applicable to every employee.

## 2. Password & Authentication Standards [MANDATORY — ALL ROLES]
Passwords must be at least 12 characters, include upper/lower case, numbers, and symbols, and must not be reused across systems. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all systems handling customer or financial data.

## 3. Acceptable Use of Company Systems
Company systems must be used for business purposes. Installing unapproved software or connecting unauthorized devices is prohibited without IT approval.

## 4. Customer & Company Data Handling [ROLE-SPECIFIC]
- **Software Support Engineer**, **Team Leader/Tech Lead**: may access production customer data only through approved, logged tooling — never via direct database export.
- **Data Analyst**: must use anonymized datasets for analysis unless explicitly authorized under DOC-05 §4.

## 5. Incident Reporting & Breach Escalation [MANDATORY]
Any suspected security incident (lost device, phishing, unauthorized access) must be reported to IT Security within **1 hour** of discovery. IT Security must notify the Data Privacy Officer within 24 hours for any incident involving personal data, per Data Privacy Policy (**DOC-05 §7**).

## 6. Remote Access & Device Security
Remote access requires company VPN and an approved, encrypted device. Personal devices may only access email and calendar, not customer or financial systems.

## 7. Optional Recommendation [OPTIONAL]
Employees are encouraged, but not required, to use a company-approved password manager for personal account hygiene.

## 8. Cross-References
- Data breach notification: DOC-05 §7
- Disciplinary escalation for violations: DOC-04 §6
