# Role Description — Branch Manager
**FourAngryBirds EdTech & HR Solutions JSC — Branch Management**

## 1. Role Summary
The Branch Manager holds final branch-level approval authority across sales, finance, and customer-escalation processes, and oversees disciplinary and security-incident matters for the branch.

## 2. Core Responsibilities
- Approve large sales deals and reimbursement claims above defined thresholds.
- Hold final authority on Tier 3 customer escalations.
- Enforce the disciplinary process, including the security-violation fast path.
- Oversee branch-level security-incident reporting compliance.
- Represent the Company professionally in client-facing engagements.

## 3. Required Competencies
Client-facing presentation standard, deal-approval authority, reimbursement approval authority, Tier-3 escalation authority, disciplinary process oversight, branch security-incident oversight.

## 4. Governing Documents
- Workplace Conduct Policy (**DOC-04**, §4.2 dress code, §6 disciplinary process).
- SOP – Sales Pipeline Management (**DOC-08**, §5 approval threshold).
- SOP – Financial Reimbursement (**DOC-09**, §4 approval matrix).
- SOP – Customer Escalation Process (**DOC-07**, §4.3 Tier 3).
- Information Security Policy (**DOC-06**, §5 incident reporting).
- Employee Handbook (**DOC-01**) and HR Leave Policy (**DOC-03**, seniority entitlement).

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions
**Q: Does every disciplinary case go through a verbal warning first?**
A: No — R061 requires security-related violations to skip straight to a written warning; only non-security violations follow the full three-stage process.

**Q: Can a Team Leader approve a Tier 3 escalation instead of me?**
A: No — R060 assigns final Tier 3 authority specifically to Branch Manager, including compensation/refund approval.
