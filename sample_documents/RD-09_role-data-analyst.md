# Role Description — Data Analyst
**FourAngryBirds EdTech & HR Solutions JSC — Data Department**

## 1. Role Summary
The Data Analyst produces analysis and reporting from company datasets, operating under strict least-privilege and anonymization defaults. Reports to **Branch Manager**.

## 2. Core Responsibilities
- Work from anonymized/aggregated datasets by default.
- Request written Data Privacy Officer approval before using non-anonymized data.
- Follow data retention and deletion schedules for analysis datasets.
- Secure access to data systems with strong authentication and approved remote access.

## 3. Required Competencies
Least-privilege data access (Data), anonymized-data-first analysis practice, data-retention compliance, systems access security, remote access security, third-party data-sharing compliance.

## 4. Governing Documents
- Data Privacy Policy (**DOC-05**, §4 access roles, §5 retention, §6 third-party sharing).
- Information Security Policy (**DOC-06**, §2 password/MFA, §4 data handling, §6 remote access).
- Employee Handbook (**DOC-01**) and HR Leave Policy (**DOC-03**).

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions
**Q: I need raw (non-anonymized) customer data for a one-off analysis — can I just pull it myself?**
A: No — R065 requires written Data Privacy Officer approval before accessing anything beyond anonymized/aggregated datasets.

**Q: A partner organization wants our aggregated analysis results — can I send them directly?**
A: Only under a signed data-processing agreement with Data Privacy Officer sign-off (R070), even for aggregated outputs derived from personal data.

## 7. Escalation & Reporting Line
Data Analyst → **Branch Manager** (branch-level reporting); data-access exceptions route through HR Executive as Data Privacy Officer.
