# SOP – Financial Reimbursement & Expense Claims

## 1. Eligible Business Expenses
Eligible expenses include business travel, client meals, conference fees, and pre-approved operational supplies.

## 2. Submission Deadlines
Travel expense claims with itemized tax invoices (VAT invoices) must be submitted within 15 calendar days of trip completion.

## 3. Invoicing Requirements
All receipts must be official e-invoices with the company tax ID (MST) and full corporate name.

## 4. Approval Thresholds [MANDATORY]
Expense claims exceeding 5,000,000 VND must be routed to the Department Head for approval before submitting to Finance.

## 5. Reimbursement Payment Cycles
Reimbursements are processed twice monthly: on the 10th and 25th via direct bank transfer.
