# SOP – Sales Pipeline Management

## 1. Overview & Pipeline Stages
The B2B sales pipeline consists of 6 stages: Lead, Qualified, Demo Scheduled, Proposal Sent, Negotiation, Closed-Won.

## 2. Lead Generation & Inbound Triage
Leads generated through marketing campaigns are assigned to Sales Executives based on industry vertical and territory.

## 3. Lead Qualification Standard [MANDATORY]
Sales Executives must confirm budget authority, a timeline within 6 months, and a genuine training/HR-tech need before marking a lead as Qualified in the CRM.

## 4. CRM Interaction Logging [MANDATORY]
Sales Executives must log every customer interaction (calls, emails, demo notes, objections) in the CRM within 24 hours.

## 5. High-Value Deal Approval Threshold [MANDATORY]
Deals with a total contract value of 500,000,000 VND or more must be routed to the Branch Manager for formal review and approval before sending the proposal to the client.

## 6. Closing & Customer Success Handover
Upon contract signature, a formal onboarding kickoff meeting is scheduled with the Customer Success team within 3 business days.

## 7. Upsell Playbook for Contract Renewals [OPTIONAL]
When conducting annual renewal conversations, Sales Executives should consult the internal upsell playbook to identify expansion opportunities.
