# SOP – Customer Escalation Process

## 1. Objective
Establish standard response times and clear escalation pathways for customer inquiries and complaints.

## 2. Support Channels & Triage
Customer requests arrive via ticketing system, email, hotline, and in-app chat. Each request is assigned a priority from P1 to P4.

## 3. Support Tiers Overview
Support is structured into Tier 1 (Frontline Helpdesk) and Tier 2 (Technical Specialists & Team Leaders).

## 4. Service Level Agreements & Escalation Thresholds

### 4.1 Tier-1 First Response SLA [MANDATORY]
Customer Support Executives must acknowledge and attempt first-contact resolution on any incoming customer issue within 2 business hours.

### 4.2 Tier-2 Escalation Trigger [MANDATORY]
Any unresolved complaint exceeding 24 hours, or involving safety, legal, or financial risk, must be escalated to a Team Leader within 1 business hour of the threshold being identified.

## 5. Escalation Logging Requirements [MANDATORY]
Every escalated case must be documented in the ticketing portal with ticket ID, customer ID, tier reached, resolution details, and total time-to-resolution.
