# Role Description — Team Leader / Tech Lead
**FourAngryBirds EdTech & HR Solutions JSC — Engineering Department**

## 1. Role Summary
The Team Leader/Tech Lead holds Tier 2 customer-escalation authority and deployment sign-off authority, and oversees the Software Support Engineer's work. Reports to **Branch Manager** for branch matters.

## 2. Core Responsibilities
- Receive and resolve Tier 2 customer escalations within the defined SLA, or advance to Tier 3.
- Review and record sign-off on every production deployment before it proceeds.
- Provide oversight of the Software Support Engineer's pre-deployment checklist and rollback execution.
- Apply the security-violation disciplinary fast path within the team.

## 3. Required Competencies
Tier-2 escalation resolution authority, deployment approval authority, deployment quality oversight, secure production-data access, systems access security, team-level disciplinary escalation awareness.

## 4. Governing Documents
- SOP – Customer Escalation Process (**DOC-07**, §4.2 Tier 2).
- SOP – Software Deployment Workflow (**DOC-10**, §2 sign-off responsibility, §4 approval gate).
- Information Security Policy (**DOC-06**, §2 password/MFA, §4 data handling).
- Workplace Conduct Policy (**DOC-04**, §6 disciplinary process).
- Employee Handbook (**DOC-01**) and HR Leave Policy (**DOC-03**, seniority entitlement).

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions
**Q: Can a deployment go live if I'm unavailable to sign off?**
A: No — R074 requires Team Leader sign-off on every production deployment; an alternate approver must be designated in advance if you're unavailable.

**Q: If a Tier 2 escalation isn't resolved after 30 hours, do I still have time?**
A: Yes, up to the 48-hour mark (R073) before it must advance to Branch Manager at Tier 3 — but earlier resolution is always preferable.

## 7. Escalation & Reporting Line
Team Leader/Tech Lead → **Branch Manager** (Tier 3 escalations, branch-level disciplinary matters); technical-architecture matters route to Head of Engineering HQ (outside this system's scope).
