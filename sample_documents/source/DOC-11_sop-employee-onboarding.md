# SOP – Employee Onboarding Process

## 1. Purpose

This SOP defines how every new employee of FourAngryBirds EdTech & HR Solutions JSC ("the Company") is prepared for, welcomed, trained and evaluated during their first 90 days. It turns the probation rule of the Employee Handbook (DOC-01 §3.2) into concrete steps, deadlines and owners, so that no new hire depends on the memory of an individual manager to receive the same start as everyone else.

The process is delivered through the SkillSprint AI platform: every onboarding learning path shown to a new hire is generated from approved company documents, checked by a Reviewer and published to the new hire's department or job position before the first working day.

## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to all permanent and fixed-term employees in every department and at both offices (Ho Chi Minh City headquarters and the Da Nang office). Interns and contractors follow Section 9 instead of the full process. Internal transfers and promotions do not repeat onboarding; they follow the promotion learning path defined by HR for the new role.

## 3. Roles & Responsibilities [ROLE-SPECIFIC]

- **HR Executive**: owns the process end to end, prepares the employment file, schedules orientation, assigns the onboarding learning path and tracks completion on the Employee Learning Dashboard.
- **Direct manager**: defines the role goals for the first 90 days, holds the 30-, 60- and 90-day check-ins and signs the probation evaluation.
- **Onboarding buddy**: an experienced colleague from the same team who answers day-to-day questions during the first 30 days (see Section 8).
- **IT Support**: creates accounts, issues equipment and enforces the security set-up required by the Information Security Policy (DOC-06).
- **Reviewer (Learning & Development)**: approves the learning path before it is published to the new hire.
- **New employee**: completes every mandatory task, lesson and assessment within the deadlines of this SOP.

## 4. Pre-Arrival Preparation

### 4.1 Five Business Days Before the Start Date [MANDATORY]

The HR Executive must complete the following at least 5 business days before the new hire's first day:

- Send the signed employment contract and the Employee Handbook (DOC-01) to the new hire's personal email.
- Collect personal data for the employment file only for the stated purpose of employment, with consent recorded as required by the Data Privacy Policy (DOC-05 §3).
- Request accounts and equipment from IT Support through the service desk.
- Confirm that a published onboarding learning path exists for the new hire's job position; if none exists, request one from the Reviewer immediately.
- Nominate an onboarding buddy and inform the direct manager.

### 4.2 Accounts & Equipment [MANDATORY]

IT Support must issue the laptop, company email and SkillSprint AI account no later than 1 business day before the start date. Multi-factor authentication must be enabled before the first login to any system that handles customer or financial data, as required by DOC-06 §2. Accounts are created with the default access of the job position only; additional access follows the least-privilege rules of DOC-05 §4.

## 5. Day 1 – Orientation

### 5.1 Welcome Session

The new hire joins a two-hour welcome session led by the HR Executive, covering company history, the organisation chart of the nine functions listed in DOC-01 §2, office facilities and the working-hours rule of DOC-01 §3.1 (08:30–17:30, core hours 09:00–16:00).

### 5.2 Mandatory Day-1 Actions [MANDATORY — ALL ROLES]

- Log in to SkillSprint AI and open the assigned onboarding learning path.
- Start the digital acknowledgement of the Employee Handbook, which must be completed within 5 business days of joining (DOC-01 §9).
- Meet the direct manager to agree on the goals for the first 90 days.
- Meet the onboarding buddy.

## 6. Learning Path Milestones

The onboarding learning path is organised into stages that match the milestones below. A later stage opens only when every module of the previous stage is complete, so the new hire learns company foundations before department practice.

### 6.1 Week 1 – Company Foundations [MANDATORY — ALL ROLES]

During Week 1 the new hire completes the modules built from the Employee Handbook (DOC-01), the Workplace Conduct Policy (DOC-04) and the role's department policy. A module is complete when every lesson is read, every work task is ticked and the module quiz is passed with a score of at least 70 percent. Quizzes may be retaken; the best score counts.

### 6.2 Week 1 – Role-Critical Training [ROLE-SPECIFIC]

Some roles must complete role-critical training before working unsupervised:

- **Customer Support Executive**: Tier 2 Escalation Authority training during Week 1 (DOC-07 §4.2, requirement R001).
- **Software Support Engineer**: the Pre-Deployment Checklist and Deployment Approval Gate modules (DOC-10 §3–§4) before joining any production release.
- **Finance Associate**: the Approval Matrix module of the Financial Reimbursement SOP (DOC-09 §4) before verifying any claim.
- **Sales Executive**: the Lead Qualification and CRM Data Entry modules (DOC-08 §3–§4) before contacting customers.

### 6.3 First 30 Days – Security & Privacy [MANDATORY — ALL ROLES]

Information Security Policy (DOC-06) and Data Privacy Policy (DOC-05) training must be completed within the first 30 days of employment, as required by DOC-01 §7. Roles that handle personal data (Data Analyst, HR Executive, Finance Associate) receive production data access only after both modules are passed.

### 6.4 Days 31–90 – Role Practice

From day 31 the new hire works through the department SOP modules of the learning path (for example DOC-07, DOC-08, DOC-09 or DOC-10) and the final assessment in the last stage. The final assessment combines questions from every module of the path.

## 7. Check-ins & Probation Evaluation

### 7.1 Check-in Schedule [MANDATORY]

The direct manager holds three documented check-ins:

- **Day 30**: review learning progress on the Employee Learning Dashboard and confirm that the security and privacy modules are complete.
- **Day 45**: mid-probation feedback; any performance concern must be written down and shared with the new hire and the HR Executive.
- **Day 90**: review of the 90-day goals and of the completed learning path.

### 7.2 Probation Evaluation [MANDATORY]

The probation period is 60 days (DOC-01 §3.2). The direct manager must submit the probation evaluation form to the HR Executive no later than day 55, so that the confirmation or non-confirmation decision is communicated before day 60. A new hire whose mandatory learning modules are incomplete on day 55 cannot receive a "confirmed" rating unless the delay was caused by the Company (for example missing accounts).

### 7.3 Extension

Probation may be extended once, by at most 30 days, with the written agreement of the new hire, the direct manager and the HR Executive. The reason for the extension and the remaining learning tasks must be recorded in the employment file.

## 8. Buddy Programme [OPTIONAL]

Teams are encouraged, but not required, to assign an onboarding buddy for the first 30 days. The buddy should meet the new hire at least once a week, is not responsible for performance evaluation, and must not share credentials or grant system access on the new hire's behalf.

## 9. Interns, Contractors & Rehires [EXCEPTION]

- **Interns** complete Week 1 company foundations and the security and privacy modules only; the probation evaluation does not apply.
- **Contractors** complete the security and privacy modules before receiving any system access; they do not receive a full onboarding learning path.
- **Rehires** returning within 12 months of leaving repeat only the modules whose source documents changed since their last day; HR regenerates the path from the current document versions.

## 10. Leave Balance Activation

The annual leave balance is activated on the first day of employment and accrues at 1 day per month, as defined in the HR Leave Policy (DOC-03 §2). Probationary employees may use accrued annual leave with manager approval but may not request unpaid leave during the first 60 days (DOC-03 §5). Carryover follows DOC-01 §6 only.

## 11. Escalation of Overdue Onboarding Tasks

- A mandatory task that is 3 business days overdue triggers an automatic reminder to the new hire and the direct manager.
- A task that is 5 business days overdue is escalated by the HR Executive to the department head.
- Missing accounts or equipment are escalated by the HR Executive to IT Support the same day and do not count against the new hire.

## 12. Records & Data Retention

The onboarding checklist, check-in notes and probation evaluation are part of the employment file. They are retained for 5 years after the end of employment, in line with the Data Privacy Policy (DOC-05 §5). Learning progress and quiz scores on SkillSprint AI are personal data and may be viewed only by the new hire, the direct manager and the HR Executive.

## 13. Onboarding KPIs

HR reports the following indicators to management every quarter:

- Share of new hires whose accounts and equipment were ready on day 1 (target: 100 percent).
- Share of new hires who completed the Week 1 foundations stage within Week 1 (target: at least 90 percent).
- Share of probation evaluations submitted by day 55 (target: 100 percent).
- Average first-attempt quiz score in the onboarding final assessment.
