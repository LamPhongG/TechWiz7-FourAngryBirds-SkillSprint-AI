# Department-Specific Exceptions

## 1. Purpose

Company-wide policies set one rule for everyone. Some departments cannot work under that rule without harming customers or breaking a legal obligation. This document is the single register of those approved exceptions. An exception that is not listed here does not exist, even if a manager has allowed it informally.

## 2. Precedence Rules [MANDATORY]

- A listed exception replaces the company-wide rule it names, but only for the employees, situations and period stated in the exception.
- Everything that an exception does not mention stays governed by the company-wide policy.
- An exception can never relax a legal requirement, a security control on customer data, or the approval limits for money.
- If two exceptions appear to overlap, the narrower one applies; if still unclear, the HR Manager decides and updates this register within 10 business days.

## 3. Customer Support – Extended Service Hours [EXCEPTION]

- **Base rule**: standard working hours 08:30–17:30, Monday to Friday (DOC-01 §3.1).
- **Exception**: Customer Support Executives work rotating shifts covering 08:00–20:00 on weekdays and 09:00–13:00 on Saturdays. Each shift is 8 hours including a 1-hour break.
- **Scope**: Customer Support teams at both offices.
- **Conditions**: the 2-business-hour acknowledgement target of DOC-07 §4.1 is measured within the hours of the shift that receives the issue. Saturday shifts are compensated by a weekday off in the same or the following week.
- **Approved by**: Branch Managers. **Valid until**: 31 December 2026, then reviewed.

## 4. Engineering & Support – On-Call Rotation [EXCEPTION]

- **Base rule**: work is performed during standard working hours (DOC-01 §3.1).
- **Exception**: Software Support Engineers and Team Leaders take part in a weekly on-call rotation outside working hours for incidents that affect all customers. On-call engineers must respond to a page within 30 minutes.
- **Scope**: Engineering teams that run customer-facing systems.
- **Conditions**: on-call staff may work from home during their on-call week using the company VPN and an approved encrypted device (DOC-06 §6); production data access still uses approved, logged tooling only (DOC-06 §4). Each on-call week earns one additional day off.
- **Approved by**: Head of Engineering. **Valid until**: no end date; reviewed yearly.

## 5. Finance – Month-End Leave Blackout [EXCEPTION]

- **Base rule**: leave is requested through the portal and approved by the direct manager within 2 business days (DOC-03 §6).
- **Exception**: Finance Associates may not take planned annual leave during the last 3 business days of each month, when the month-end close is performed (DOC-15 §4.4).
- **Scope**: Finance team members involved in the close.
- **Conditions**: sick leave and statutory leave are never blocked by this exception (DOC-03 §3, §4). Leave already approved before this register was published is honoured.
- **Approved by**: Finance Manager. **Valid until**: no end date.

## 6. Sales – Client Entertainment Pre-Approval [EXCEPTION]

- **Base rule**: expenses are claimed after the fact and approved according to the amount (DOC-09 §4).
- **Exception**: Sales Executives may spend up to 2,000,000 VND per client meeting on meals with a prospect or customer without asking for approval in advance.
- **Scope**: Sales Executives with an active opportunity in the CRM for that client.
- **Conditions**: the claim still goes through DOC-09 within 15 calendar days, with the CRM opportunity ID; the 5,000,000 VND Branch Manager threshold of DOC-09 §4 still applies to the monthly total per employee.
- **Approved by**: Head of Sales & Marketing. **Valid until**: 31 December 2026.

## 7. Data – Access to Identifiable Data for Fraud Review [EXCEPTION]

- **Base rule**: Data Analysts work on anonymised or aggregated datasets by default (DOC-05 §4, DOC-06 §4).
- **Exception**: a named Data Analyst may access identifiable payment records to investigate suspected reimbursement fraud or duplicate payments.
- **Scope**: one Data Analyst per investigation, named in writing.
- **Conditions**: written approval from the Data Privacy Officer before access; access is limited to 10 business days per investigation, is logged, and the working extract is deleted when the investigation closes. Findings are reported to the Finance Manager only.
- **Approved by**: Data Privacy Officer and Finance Manager jointly. **Valid until**: per investigation.

## 8. Operations – Field Visit Attire and Hours [EXCEPTION]

- **Base rule**: business-casual dress code and standard working hours (DOC-04 §4.1, DOC-01 §3.1).
- **Exception**: Operations Coordinators on partner-school visits are exempt from the office dress code while on site (DOC-04 §7) and may start or finish outside standard hours to match the school timetable.
- **Scope**: days with a field visit planned on the branch calendar (DOC-12 §6.2).
- **Conditions**: professional behaviour standards still apply (DOC-04 §2). Hours worked beyond 8 on a visit day are recorded and taken back within the same month.
- **Approved by**: Branch Manager. **Valid until**: no end date.

## 9. Da Nang Office – Purchase Approval [EXCEPTION]

- **Base rule**: the Branch Manager approves purchase orders up to 50,000,000 VND (DOC-12 §3.1).
- **Exception**: until the Da Nang office has a full-time Branch Manager, purchase orders for that office up to 20,000,000 VND are approved by the headquarters Branch Manager remotely, and larger orders go directly to the Chief Operating Officer.
- **Scope**: Da Nang office only.
- **Conditions**: expense-claim thresholds of DOC-09 §4 are unchanged.
- **Approved by**: Chief Operating Officer. **Valid until**: the appointment of a Da Nang Branch Manager.

## 10. Requesting a New Exception

- The department head submits a request to the HR Manager stating the base rule, the proposed exception, the scope, the reason and the proposed end date.
- HR checks the request against the precedence rules of Section 2; requests that would relax a legal requirement, a customer-data security control or a money approval limit are rejected.
- Approved exceptions are added to this register with a new version number and announced to the affected employees. Learning paths that cite the base rule are regenerated so that employees in scope learn the exception.

## 11. Review

The HR Manager reviews every exception at least once a year and removes exceptions whose end date has passed. An expired exception stops applying on its end date even if this register has not yet been updated.
