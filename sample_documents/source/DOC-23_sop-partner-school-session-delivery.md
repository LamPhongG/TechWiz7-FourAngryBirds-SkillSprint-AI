# SOP – Partner-School Session Delivery

## 1. Purpose

This SOP describes how the Operations team prepares, runs and follows up training sessions delivered at partner schools. It details the delivery part of the Operations Coordinator duties in DOC-12 §6. Onboarding a new school (DOC-12 §6.1) and ending a partnership (DOC-12 §6.5) are covered by DOC-12 and are not repeated here.

## 2. Scope

The SOP applies to every scheduled session at a partner school, whether it is delivered on site, online through the learning platform, or in a mixed format. It applies at both offices, Ho Chi Minh City and Da Nang.

## 3. Roles

- **Operations Coordinator**: owns the delivery calendar, prepares each session, is the school's contact on the day and writes the visit report.
- **Trainer**: delivers the content and confirms the session plan. Trainers may be employees or contracted vendors (DOC-24).
- **Branch Manager**: approves the delivery calendar, purchase orders and any change that affects the contract with the school.
- **Customer Support Executive**: handles issues reported by the school through the escalation process of DOC-07.
- **Software Support Engineer**: supports platform problems that Customer Support cannot solve at Tier 1.

## 4. Delivery Calendar

- Every session is entered in the operations calendar at least 10 business days before it takes place, with the school, location, trainer, format and number of learners.
- The calendar is shared with the Branch Manager (DOC-12 §6.1) and reviewed every Monday by the Operations Coordinator.
- A session that needs a field visit is also planned on the branch calendar at least 3 business days in advance (DOC-12 §6.2).
- A school may move a session free of charge up to 5 business days before the date. Later changes are recorded as a change request and approved by the Branch Manager.

## 5. Session Preparation [MANDATORY]

### 5.1 Five Business Days Before

- Confirm the room, equipment and internet connection with the school's contact person in writing.
- Confirm the trainer and send the trainer the session plan, the learner list size (not the names) and the school's rules for visitors.
- Order printed materials, transport or catering through DOC-24 when they are needed, and check that the purchase order is approved within the Branch Manager's limit (DOC-12 §3.1).
- Check that learner accounts exist on the learning platform and that the school's data-processing agreement is on file (DOC-05 §6).

### 5.2 One Business Day Before

- Run the readiness checklist: platform login tested with a trainer account, session content published, attendance sheet prepared, emergency contact numbers of the school saved.
- Confirm the arrival time with the school and the trainer.
- If any check fails and cannot be fixed before the session, inform the Branch Manager the same day and agree with the school whether to postpone.

## 6. Day-of-Session Checklist

- Arrive at least 45 minutes before the start time for on-site sessions.
- Access Company systems only through the company VPN on an approved, encrypted device (DOC-06 §6). School computers are never used to log in to Company systems.
- The office dress code does not apply on site, but the professional behaviour standards of DOC-04 §2 do (DOC-04 §7, DOC-20 §8).
- Start the session on time, record attendance and note any learner who needs follow-up.
- Collect the paper attendance sheet at the end of the session; it is scanned and then destroyed within 5 business days.

## 7. Attendance and Learner Data [MANDATORY]

- Learner data is used only for the purpose stated in the school's contract (DOC-05 §3).
- Attendance is recorded on the learning platform within 2 business days of the session.
- Photos of learners are taken only when the school has confirmed written consent for that session, and they are stored in the Company's shared drive, never on a personal phone.
- Lists of learner names are never sent by chat applications or personal email. They are shared through the learning platform or the Company's file storage only.
- A lost attendance sheet or device is a security incident and is reported to IT Security within 1 hour (DOC-06 §5).

## 8. Handling Disruptions

### 8.1 Platform or Connection Problems

When the learning platform is unavailable during a scheduled session, the Operations Coordinator logs a customer issue and it is handled through the escalation process (DOC-07 §4). The school is informed of the expected resolution time within 2 business hours (DOC-12 §6.4). If the problem affects all customers, Customer Support contacts the Engineering on-call engineer (DOC-20 §4).

### 8.2 Trainer Absence

If the trainer cannot attend, the Operations Coordinator first looks for a replacement trainer with the same content qualification. If no replacement is found by the day before, the session is postponed and a new date is offered within 10 business days.

### 8.3 Rescheduling and Complaints

Every postponed session is recorded in the delivery log with the reason. A complaint from the school about a session is passed to Customer Support and handled through DOC-07; the Operations Coordinator provides the facts but does not promise compensation, which is decided by the Branch Manager at Tier 3 (DOC-07 §4.3).

## 9. Visit Report and Follow-Up

The visit report is submitted within 1 business day of the visit (DOC-12 §6.2). It lists the attendees, the issues raised and the follow-up actions with an owner and a due date. Actions that concern the platform are logged as customer issues; actions that concern the contract are passed to the Branch Manager.

## 10. Safety on Site

- Follow the school's safety rules and sign in at reception on arrival.
- Know the location of the emergency exits of the room used for the session.
- An injury or safety incident involving a learner, trainer or employee is reported to the school at once and to the Branch Manager the same day. It is escalated as a safety risk through DOC-07 §4.2 when it involves a customer.

## 11. Monthly Delivery Review

In the monthly branch review (DOC-12 §5.2) the Operations Coordinator presents the delivery figures of the month:

- Sessions planned, delivered, postponed and cancelled, with reasons.
- Share of visit reports submitted within 1 business day.
- Open follow-up actions older than 10 business days.
- Vendor issues for the vendor review of DOC-24 §8.

## 12. Optional Practice [OPTIONAL]

A short feedback form for learners at the end of each session is encouraged. When it is used, it is anonymous by default.
