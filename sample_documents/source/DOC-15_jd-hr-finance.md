# HR & Finance Role Descriptions

## 1. Purpose

This document describes the two support-function roles that handle the most sensitive personal and financial data of the Company: HR Executive and Finance Associate. It states the mission, responsibilities, decision limits, data-access rules and performance indicators of each role. It is the reference for recruitment, onboarding learning paths (DOC-11) and probation reviews.

## 2. Why These Roles Carry Extra Controls

Both roles see personal data that other employees do not: employment files, salaries, bank details, medical certificates and payment records. Mistakes in these roles directly affect colleagues and customers, and several of their tasks have statutory deadlines under Vietnamese labour and social-insurance law. For this reason both roles receive production access only after the security and privacy modules are passed (DOC-11 §6.3) and are included in the quarterly access review.

## 3. HR Executive

### 3.1 Mission

Recruit, onboard and support employees fairly and on time, keep employment records accurate and confidential, and act as the Company's Data Privacy Officer.

### 3.2 Key Responsibilities [MANDATORY]

- Own the onboarding process end to end: prepare the employment file 5 business days before the start date, assign the onboarding learning path and track completion (DOC-11 §3–§4).
- Record approved leave and update entitlement balances after manager approval (DOC-03 §6).
- Verify eligibility for maternity and paternity leave and file social-insurance paperwork within statutory deadlines (DOC-03 §4).
- Receive and handle harassment and discrimination reports immediately (DOC-04 §3).
- Receive conflict-of-interest disclosures, which employees must submit within 10 business days of the conflict arising (DOC-04 §5).
- Collect the probation evaluation from the direct manager by day 55 and communicate the decision before day 60 (DOC-11 §7.2).

### 3.3 Data Privacy Officer Duties [ROLE-SPECIFIC: HR Executive]

A designated HR Executive acts as the Data Privacy Officer (DPO) described in DOC-05 §4. The DPO:

- Approves in writing any data access beyond the default access of a role (DOC-05 §4).
- Signs off every data-processing agreement before personal data is shared with a third party (DOC-05 §6).
- Notifies affected individuals within statutory deadlines after a confirmed breach (DOC-05 §7), based on the incident reported by IT Security within 24 hours (DOC-06 §5).
- Leads the quarterly access review with IT Security and records its findings.

### 3.4 Data Access Rules

- Access to employee personal records is limited to HR-necessary fields (DOC-05 §4).
- Medical certificates for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive working days (DOC-03 §3) are stored in the restricted HR folder and are never attached to email.
- Employment files are retained for 5 years after the end of employment (DOC-05 §5).

### 3.5 Performance Indicators

- Share of new hires with accounts and equipment ready on day 1 (DOC-11 §13).
- Share of social-insurance filings made within the statutory deadline (target: 100 percent).
- Average number of days to close a harassment or discrimination report.

## 4. Finance Associate

### 4.1 Mission

Process payments and reimbursements accurately and on time, and make sure that every payment is supported, approved and compliant.

### 4.2 Key Responsibilities [MANDATORY]

- Verify every expense claim for policy compliance, checking that original receipts and a completed form were submitted within 15 calendar days of the expense (DOC-09 §3).
- Reject automatically any claim submitted after 30 days and state the reason in writing (DOC-09 §3, §6).
- Check that claims of 5,000,000 VND or more carry both the direct manager's and the Branch Manager's approval (DOC-09 §4).
- Pay approved claims within 10 business days of verification (DOC-09 §5).
- Verify vendor invoices forwarded by the Operations Coordinator against the approved purchase order (DOC-12 §6.3).
- Store receipt images according to the retention rules of DOC-05 §5, not indefinitely (DOC-09 §7).

### 4.3 Decision Limits [ROLE-SPECIFIC: Finance Associate]

- May reject a claim that does not meet DOC-09; may not approve a claim on behalf of a manager.
- May not change supplier bank details without a call-back verification to a phone number already on file; a change request received only by email is treated as a possible phishing incident and reported to IT Security within 1 hour (DOC-06 §5).
- Access is limited to payment and reimbursement data (DOC-05 §4).

### 4.4 Month-End Close

The Finance Associate completes bank reconciliation and the accruals list during the last 3 business days of each month. Leave during this period is subject to the Finance month-end exception described in DOC-20.

### 4.5 Performance Indicators

- Share of approved claims paid within 10 business days.
- Number of payments made without complete approval (target: zero).
- Month-end close completed by the third business day of the following month.

## 5. Shared Rules for HR & Finance

- Never discuss salary, medical or bank information in open channels or shared calendars.
- Lock the screen whenever leaving the workstation; paper files with personal data stay in locked cabinets (DOC-12 §4.2).
- Both roles follow the standard annual leave entitlement of 12 working days (DOC-03 §2).
- Both roles complete the security and privacy modules within the first 30 days and before production access (DOC-11 §6.3).

## 6. Segregation of Duties [MANDATORY]

No single person may both approve and pay the same claim or invoice. An HR Executive may not approve changes to their own employment record, and a Finance Associate may not verify their own expense claim; such items are assigned to another member of the team or to the team manager.

## 7. Career Path

- HR Executive → Senior HR Executive → HR Manager.
- Finance Associate → Senior Accountant → Finance Manager.
