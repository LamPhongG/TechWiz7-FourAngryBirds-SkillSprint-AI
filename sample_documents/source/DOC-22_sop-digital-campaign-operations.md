# SOP – Digital Campaign Operations

## 1. Purpose

This SOP describes how the Marketing team plans, launches, runs and closes digital campaigns: search and social advertising, email campaigns, webinars and events. It turns the Marketing Executive responsibilities of DOC-13 §4 into day-to-day steps. Where this SOP and DOC-13 differ, DOC-13 prevails and this SOP is corrected.

## 2. Scope and Roles

This SOP applies to every campaign that is paid for by the Company or that sends messages to people outside the Company.

- **Marketing Executive**: prepares the brief, builds and launches the campaign, monitors it and reports results.
- **Head of Sales & Marketing**: approves briefs and the annual marketing plan.
- **Branch Manager**: approves campaigns with a budget of 50,000,000 VND or more (DOC-13 §4.3).
- **Data Privacy Officer** (a designated HR Executive): signs off every campaign that sends personal data to an external platform (DOC-05 §6).
- **Sales Executive**: receives and qualifies campaign leads (DOC-08 §3).
- **Data Analyst**: provides the monthly campaign data set and the metric definitions of DOC-26.

## 3. Campaign Planning

### 3.1 Annual and Quarterly Plan

The Marketing Executive drafts the annual marketing plan in November for the following year. The plan lists the target segments, the main campaigns per quarter, the always-on advertising budget and the expected number of qualified leads. The Head of Sales & Marketing approves the plan before 15 December. At the start of each quarter the plan is reviewed against the results of the previous quarter and adjusted.

### 3.2 Campaign Calendar

Every approved campaign is entered in the shared campaign calendar with its launch date, channels and owner. Campaigns that target the same segment are spaced at least 7 calendar days apart so that the same contacts do not receive several messages in one week.

## 4. Campaign Brief and Approval [MANDATORY]

No campaign may go live without a recorded approval. The approval workflow is the four-step workflow of DOC-13 §4.3; this section adds the practical details.

- The brief uses the campaign brief template and states the audience, channels, budget, key message, call to action, success metric and planned end date.
- Every statistic or result quoted in the creative assets is listed in the brief with its internal source (DOC-13 §4.5, DOC-21 §6).
- The brief names the data flows: which contact list is used, which platform receives it and whether a data-processing agreement exists.
- The Head of Sales & Marketing records the approval in the campaign log. For a budget of 50,000,000 VND or more, the Branch Manager's approval is recorded as well before launch.
- Creative assets are checked against the brand and content rules of DOC-21 before launch.

A campaign that goes live without a complete approval record is paused as soon as it is found and handled as a policy violation under DOC-04 §6.

## 5. Audience Data and Consent [MANDATORY]

- Only contacts with a recorded consent for marketing messages may receive email campaigns (DOC-05 §3). The consent date and source must be visible in the CRM.
- Contact lists are exported from the CRM only for the approved campaign and only to a platform with a signed data-processing agreement and Data Privacy Officer sign-off (DOC-05 §6, DOC-13 §4.6).
- Exported lists are deleted from local folders once the upload to the platform is confirmed. Contact lists must never be stored on personal devices or personal accounts (DOC-06 §6).
- Lookalike or retargeting audiences built by advertising platforms use hashed data only. Uploading raw phone numbers or email addresses is not allowed.
- Marketing platforms that hold customer data use multi-factor authentication for every user (DOC-06 §2).

## 6. Channel Procedures

### 6.1 Search and Social Advertising

The Marketing Executive may run always-on advertising up to 30,000,000 VND per month within the approved annual plan (DOC-13 §4.6). Spend is checked every Monday against the monthly limit. When spend reaches 80 percent of the monthly limit before the 20th of the month, bids are reduced and the Head of Sales & Marketing is informed. Every advertising account is registered to a Company email address, never to a personal account.

### 6.2 Email Campaigns

- Every email contains the Company name in full, a working unsubscribe link and the Company's postal address.
- A test send to at least two colleagues is done before every campaign, and the links are checked.
- Unsubscribe requests are processed within 2 business days, including requests that arrive by reply rather than through the link.
- Email campaigns are not sent to more than one segment per day, and not on public holidays.
- When the complaint rate of a campaign goes above 0.3 percent, the next send to that segment is stopped until the list has been reviewed.

### 6.3 Webinars and Events

Registration forms state the purpose of the data collected and record consent at the point of collection (DOC-05 §3). Physical events that need a venue, printing or catering are booked through the Operations Coordinator and the procurement procedure of DOC-24. Speaker slides are checked against DOC-21 like any other external asset.

## 7. Lead Handover to Sales [MANDATORY]

Every campaign lead is handed over to Sales in the CRM within 1 business day, tagged with its campaign source (DOC-13 §4.2). Leads that do not meet the minimum data set (name, organisation, contact channel with consent, stated interest) are returned to the nurture list and are not passed to Sales. The Sales Executive qualifies the lead according to DOC-08 §3 and records the outcome in the CRM, so that the monthly report can calculate the cost per qualified lead.

## 8. Budget Tracking and Invoices

- Each campaign has a budget line in the marketing budget, monitored under DOC-28 §4.
- Agency and platform invoices are forwarded to the Finance Associate for verification against the approved purchase order (DOC-24 §7).
- Expenses paid personally, for example a small event purchase, are claimed through DOC-09 within 15 calendar days.
- An overspend of a campaign line above 10 percent requires a new approval of the brief before more money is spent (DOC-28 §5).

## 9. Measurement and Reporting

The Marketing Executive publishes the monthly campaign report by the 5th business day of the following month. The report shows spend, leads, qualified leads, cost per qualified lead, and the unsubscribe and complaint rates of email campaigns (DOC-13 §4.7). Figures come from the data set provided by the Data Analyst and use the definitions of DOC-26, so that Marketing and Sales report the same numbers.

## 10. Campaign Incidents

A campaign incident is any event where a message reaches the wrong audience, shows wrong information or exposes personal data.

- Stop the campaign or the sending sequence at once.
- Report the incident to IT Security within 1 hour of discovery if personal data may be involved (DOC-06 §5); IT Security informs the Data Privacy Officer within 24 hours (DOC-05 §7).
- Record in the campaign log what happened, how many contacts were affected and what was done.
- A correction message is sent only after the Head of Sales & Marketing has approved its text.

## 11. Campaign Close-Out

Within 10 business days after a campaign ends, the Marketing Executive closes it: final spend and results are recorded, unused audience files are deleted from the platforms, and a short lessons-learned note is added to the campaign log.

## 12. Optional Practice [OPTIONAL]

A/B testing of subject lines and landing pages is encouraged for campaigns with more than 2,000 recipients but is not required.
