# Remote Work & Overtime Policy (Conflicting Policy Sample)

> Test fixture. This policy was assembled from two unreconciled drafts and is kept in the document store to test contradiction detection. It must not be published to employees.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the rules for working away from the office and for work performed outside standard working hours. It aims to give teams flexibility while protecting customer service levels, employee wellbeing and the security of company data.

## 2. Scope

The policy applies to all employees who have passed probation, at both the Ho Chi Minh City headquarters and the Da Nang office. Employees on probation work from the office unless their manager approves an exception in writing.

## 3. Remote Work

### 3.1 Remote Days Allowance [MANDATORY]

Eligible employees may work remotely for a maximum of 2 days per week. Remote days must be agreed with the direct manager at least 1 business day in advance and recorded in the team calendar.

### 3.2 Availability During Remote Work

Remote employees must be reachable by chat and phone during core collaboration hours, 09:00–16:00, and must join scheduled meetings with their camera available when requested.

### 3.3 Devices for Remote Work [MANDATORY]

Remote work is allowed only on a company-issued, encrypted laptop connected through the company VPN. Personal devices must never be used to access customer or financial systems.

### 3.4 Home Office Expenses

Employees who work remotely may claim a home-office allowance for internet costs of up to 300,000 VND per month. Claims must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the end of the month, with the internet bill attached.

## 4. Overtime

### 4.1 Prior Approval [MANDATORY]

All overtime requires prior written approval from the direct manager. Work performed outside standard hours without prior approval is not recognised as overtime.

### 4.2 Limits

Overtime must not exceed 40 hours per month or 200 hours per year per employee, in line with the Vietnamese Labour Code.

### 4.3 Recording

Approved overtime is recorded in the HR portal within 2 business days of the work being performed, with the approval reference.

### 4.4 Small Overtime

Overtime of less than 10 hours per month is approved automatically and does not require prior approval from the manager, as long as it is recorded in the HR portal.

## 5. Overtime Compensation

Approved overtime on weekdays is paid at 150 percent of the normal hourly rate, overtime on weekly rest days at 200 percent, and overtime on public holidays at 300 percent.

## 6. Equipment & Support

The Company provides a headset and, on request, an external monitor for home use. Employees who prefer to work on a personal laptop may do so for remote work if up-to-date antivirus software is installed and the device is registered with IT Support.

## 7. Team-Level Arrangements

### 7.1 Customer-Facing Teams

Customer Support teams keep at least half of each shift in the office so that walk-in customers and phone lines are covered.

### 7.2 Project Teams

Project teams in Engineering, Data and Marketing may allow each member up to 3 remote days per week during project delivery phases, provided the project lead approves the schedule in advance.

## 8. Time Off in Lieu

### 8.1 Compensation Method

All overtime is compensated as time off in lieu at a rate of one hour off for each hour worked; overtime is not paid in cash.

### 8.2 Unused Leave

Unused annual leave and unused time off in lieu may be carried over into the following year, up to a total of 10 days, and must be used by 30 June.

## 9. Security Incidents During Remote Work

A lost or stolen device used for remote work must be reported to IT Security within 24 hours, together with a written description of the circumstances.

## 10. Wellbeing

Employees working remotely are encouraged to keep a regular schedule, take a lunch break away from the screen and switch off notifications outside working hours unless they are on call.

## 11. Review

This policy is reviewed every 12 months by HR together with IT Security and Finance.
