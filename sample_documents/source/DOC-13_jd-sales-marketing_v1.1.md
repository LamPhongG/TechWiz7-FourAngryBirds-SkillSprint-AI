# Job Descriptions – Sales & Marketing

## 1. Purpose

This document describes the two customer-acquisition roles of the Company: Sales Executive and Marketing Executive. For each role it states the mission, the responsibilities, the decisions the role may take alone, the performance indicators and the training that must be completed during onboarding. It is used for recruitment, onboarding learning paths (DOC-11) and performance reviews.

## 2. How to Read This Document

Responsibilities marked [MANDATORY] are part of the role's minimum standard and are assessed during probation. Items marked [OPTIONAL] are good practice. Decision limits repeat those of the source SOPs; if a source SOP changes, the SOP prevails.

## 3. Sales Executive

### 3.1 Mission

Win and grow B2B customers for the Company's EdTech and HR-solution products by qualifying demand accurately, presenting the right solution and closing deals within the approval limits of the Company.

### 3.2 Key Responsibilities [MANDATORY]

- Qualify leads using the three criteria of DOC-08 §3: budget authority, a timeline within 6 months, and a genuine training or HR-technology need. Leads that do not meet all three are closed as Closed-Lost with a reason code.
- Log every customer interaction in the CRM within 24 hours (DOC-08 §4), including the consent status of every contact entered.
- Prepare proposals using the approved price list; discounts above 10 percent require the Branch Manager's approval.
- Submit the weekly pipeline summary every Friday before 17:00 (DOC-08 §6).
- Hand every signed contract over to the Operations Coordinator within 1 business day so that partner-school onboarding can start (DOC-12 §6.1).

### 3.3 Decision Limits [ROLE-SPECIFIC: Sales Executive]

- May close deals under 500,000,000 VND directly; deals of 500,000,000 VND or more need Branch Manager approval before the proposal is sent (DOC-08 §5).
- May approve client entertainment within the limits of DOC-09 §2; claims follow the reimbursement SOP.
- May not promise refunds, compensation or service-level exceptions; these are decided at Tier 3 by the Branch Manager (DOC-07 §4.3).

### 3.4 Performance Indicators

- Quarterly revenue against target.
- Lead-to-qualified conversion rate and qualified-to-won conversion rate.
- CRM hygiene: share of interactions logged within 24 hours (target: at least 95 percent).
- Forecast accuracy of the monthly pipeline (reviewed by the Branch Manager, DOC-12 §5.2).

### 3.5 Required Training

- Week 1: Lead Qualification and CRM Data Entry modules (DOC-08 §3–§4) before contacting customers (DOC-11 §6.2).
- First 30 days: Data Privacy Policy (DOC-05) and Information Security Policy (DOC-06).
- Client-facing conduct and business-formal dress code for client meetings (DOC-04 §4.2).

### 3.6 Optional Practice [OPTIONAL]

Using the internal upsell playbook for renewal conversations is encouraged but not required (DOC-08 §7).

## 4. Marketing Executive

### 4.1 Mission

Generate qualified demand for the Company's products through digital campaigns, content and events, while protecting the brand and the personal data of every contact the Company collects.

### 4.2 Key Responsibilities [MANDATORY]

- Plan and run digital campaigns (search, social, email) and measure their contribution to qualified leads.
- Maintain the website's product pages and the resources section in cooperation with the Engineering team.
- Collect lead and contact data only for a stated purpose, with consent recorded at the point of collection (DOC-05 §3).
- Hand every campaign lead over to Sales in the CRM within 1 business day, tagged with its campaign source.
- Report campaign results monthly: spend, leads, qualified leads and cost per qualified lead.

### 4.3 Campaign Approval Workflow [MANDATORY]

Every external campaign follows four steps before launch:

- Step 1: the Marketing Executive writes a campaign brief with the audience, channels, budget, message and success metric.
- Step 2: the Head of Sales & Marketing approves the brief; campaigns with a budget of 50,000,000 VND or more also require Branch Manager approval.
- Step 3: creative assets are checked against the brand guidelines in Section 4.4 and against the claims in Section 4.5.
- Step 4: the data flows of the campaign are checked; any personal data sent to an advertising or email platform requires a signed data-processing agreement and Data Privacy Officer sign-off (DOC-05 §6).

A campaign may go live only when all four steps are recorded in the campaign log. Launching without a recorded approval is a policy violation handled under DOC-04 §6.

### 4.4 Brand Guidelines

- Use the Company name "FourAngryBirds EdTech & HR Solutions" in full at least once in every external asset.
- Use only the approved logo files and colour palette from the brand kit.
- Customer names, logos or quotes may be published only with the customer's written permission.

### 4.5 Claims & Testimonials [MANDATORY]

Marketing materials must not state results that the Company cannot prove, such as guaranteed hiring outcomes or fixed percentage improvements. Every statistic in an external asset must have a documented internal source, kept with the campaign brief.

### 4.6 Decision Limits [ROLE-SPECIFIC: Marketing Executive]

- May approve content changes to existing web pages without further approval.
- May spend up to 30,000,000 VND per month on always-on digital advertising within an approved annual plan.
- May not export CRM contact lists to any external tool without the data-processing check of Section 4.3, step 4.

### 4.7 Performance Indicators

- Qualified leads generated per month and cost per qualified lead.
- Share of campaigns launched with a complete approval record (target: 100 percent).
- Unsubscribe and complaint rates of email campaigns.

### 4.8 Required Training

- Week 1: Workplace Conduct Policy (DOC-04) and the Campaign Approval Workflow of this document.
- First 30 days: Data Privacy Policy (DOC-05) with emphasis on consent and third-party sharing, and Information Security Policy (DOC-06), including multi-factor authentication for marketing platforms that hold customer data.

## 5. Shared Rules for Sales & Marketing

- Customer contact data belongs to the Company, not to the employee; it must never be copied to personal devices or personal accounts (DOC-06 §6).
- Both roles follow the standard annual leave entitlement of 12 working days (DOC-03 §2) and the leave approval workflow of DOC-03 §6.
- Complaints received from customers or campaign contacts are passed to Customer Support and handled through DOC-07.

## 6. Career Path

- Sales Executive → Senior Sales Executive → Branch Manager.
- Marketing Executive → Senior Marketing Executive → Head of Sales & Marketing.

Promotion into a new role is supported by a promotion learning path generated from the documents of the target role and reviewed before it is published.
