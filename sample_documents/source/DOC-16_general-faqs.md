# General Company FAQs

## 1. About These FAQs

These answers summarise the questions new and current employees ask most often. Each answer names the policy it comes from. The FAQs are a guide, not a policy: if an answer here ever differs from the policy it cites, the cited policy prevails and HR corrects the FAQ.

## 2. Working Hours & Attendance

**Q: What are the normal working hours?**
Standard working hours are 08:30–17:30, Monday to Friday, with core collaboration hours of 09:00–16:00 (DOC-01 §3.1).

**Q: Can I start later than 08:30?**
Engineering and Data roles may adopt flexible start times with their manager's approval (DOC-01 §3.1). Other roles keep the standard hours unless a department exception applies (DOC-20).

**Q: Can I work from home?**
Remote access to company systems is allowed only through the company VPN on an approved, encrypted device. Personal devices may be used for email and calendar only (DOC-06 §6). How many remote days a team may take is agreed with the manager within the limits of any department exception in DOC-20.

## 3. Probation & Onboarding

**Q: How long is probation?**
Probation lasts 60 days (DOC-01 §3.2). Your manager submits your probation evaluation by day 55 (DOC-11 §7.2).

**Q: What must I finish during my first week?**
Log in to SkillSprint AI on day 1, acknowledge the Employee Handbook within 5 business days of joining (DOC-01 §9) and complete the Week 1 company foundations stage of your learning path (DOC-11 §6.1).

**Q: What score do I need to pass a module quiz?**
At least 70 percent. You can retake a quiz, and your best score counts (DOC-11 §6.1).

**Q: When must I finish security and privacy training?**
Within the first 30 days of employment (DOC-01 §7). Roles that handle personal data get production access only after both modules are passed (DOC-11 §6.3).

## 4. Leave

**Q: How many days of annual leave do I get?**
The standard entitlement is 12 working days per year, accrued at 1 day per month. Team Leader / Tech Lead and Branch Manager roles receive 15 days (DOC-03 §2, §2.1).

**Q: Can I carry unused leave into next year?**
Yes, up to 5 unused days. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March of the following year or they are forfeited (DOC-01 §6). Any older handbook that says otherwise is obsolete.

**Q: How do I request leave?**
Submit the request in the HR portal. Your direct manager approves or rejects it within 2 business days, and the HR Executive then updates your balance (DOC-03 §6).

**Q: Do I need a medical certificate for sick leave?**
Only when sick leave lasts more than 2 consecutive working days. Submit the certificate to HR within 3 business days of returning (DOC-03 §3).

**Q: How long is maternity and paternity leave?**
Maternity leave is 6 months and paternity leave is 5–14 working days depending on the circumstances of the birth (DOC-03 §4).

**Q: Can I take unpaid leave during probation?**
No. Unpaid leave is not available during the first 60 days (DOC-03 §5).

## 5. Expenses & Payments

**Q: How soon must I submit an expense claim?**
Within 15 calendar days of the expense, with original receipts and a completed form. Claims submitted after 30 days are rejected automatically (DOC-09 §3).

**Q: Who approves my claim?**
Your direct manager approves claims under 5,000,000 VND. Claims of 5,000,000 VND or more also need Branch Manager approval (DOC-09 §4).

**Q: When will I be paid back?**
Within 10 business days after the Finance Associate verifies the claim (DOC-09 §5).

**Q: My claim was rejected. What can I do?**
You may appeal once, in writing, to the Finance Associate's manager within 5 business days (DOC-09 §6).

## 6. Security & Personal Data

**Q: What password rules apply?**
At least 12 characters with upper and lower case letters, numbers and symbols, and never reused across systems. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory on systems that hold customer or financial data (DOC-06 §2).

**Q: I clicked a suspicious link. What now?**
Report it to IT Security within 1 hour of noticing (DOC-06 §5). Reporting quickly is never punished; hiding an incident is.

**Q: Can I send customer data to a partner by email?**
Only if a signed data-processing agreement exists and the Data Privacy Officer has signed off (DOC-05 §6).

**Q: How long does the Company keep my personal file?**
Employee records are kept for 5 years after employment ends (DOC-05 §5).

## 7. Conduct & Workplace

**Q: What is the dress code?**
Business-casual in all offices. Sales Executives and Branch Managers wear business-formal attire for client meetings (DOC-04 §4). Operations Coordinators on partner-school visits are exempt from the office dress code (DOC-04 §7).

**Q: How do I report harassment?**
Report it to the HR Executive immediately; reports are handled under the disciplinary process (DOC-04 §3, §6).

**Q: A relative of mine works for one of our vendors. Do I need to tell anyone?**
Yes. Disclose the potential conflict of interest to the HR Executive within 10 business days (DOC-04 §5).

## 8. Benefits

**Q: Does the Company provide health insurance?**
Yes. All employees who pass probation are enrolled in the Company's private health insurance plan from the first day of the following month, in addition to statutory social insurance.

**Q: Is there an annual health check?**
Yes. Every employee is offered one free health check per calendar year at a partner clinic; the HR Executive announces the booking window each spring.

**Q: Is there a learning budget?**
Each employee who has passed probation may use up to 5,000,000 VND per year for approved courses or certifications related to their role. Requests are approved by the direct manager and claimed through DOC-09.

**Q: Who do I contact for questions not covered here?**
Your HR Executive for employment, leave and benefits questions; IT Support for accounts and devices; the Finance Associate for payments.
