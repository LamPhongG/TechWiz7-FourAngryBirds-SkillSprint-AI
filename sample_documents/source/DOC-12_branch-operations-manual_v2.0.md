# Branch Management Operations Manual

## 1. Purpose & Scope

This manual describes how the Company's two sites — the Ho Chi Minh City headquarters and the Da Nang office opened in 2025 — are run day to day. It sets out the authority of the Branch Manager, the operational duties of the Operations Coordinator, and the routines both roles use to keep sales, customer support and partner-school programmes running. It applies to everyone who works at, or on behalf of, a branch.

Where this manual repeats an approval limit or deadline defined in another document, the other document is the authoritative source and is named in brackets.

## 2. Branch Structure

Each branch is led by a Branch Manager who reports to the Chief Operating Officer. The functions present at a branch report to the Branch Manager for day-to-day operations and to their department head for professional standards.

- **Ho Chi Minh City headquarters**: all nine functions of DOC-01 §2; hosts the central IT Security and Finance teams.
- **Da Nang office**: Sales, Customer Support, Operations and a small Engineering team; shares Finance, HR and IT Security with headquarters.

## 3. Branch Manager Authority [ROLE-SPECIFIC: Branch Manager]

### 3.1 Approval Authority

The Branch Manager holds the following approval rights. Limits are cumulative with, not a replacement for, the approvals required by the source documents.

- Sales deals of 500,000,000 VND or more before the proposal is sent (DOC-08 §5).
- Expense claims of 5,000,000 VND or more, in addition to the direct manager (DOC-09 §4).
- Compensation, refunds and service-level exceptions at Tier 3 of the customer escalation process (DOC-07 §4.3).
- Purchase orders for branch supplies and services up to 100,000,000 VND per order; larger orders require the Chief Operating Officer.

### 3.2 Delegation [MANDATORY]

When the Branch Manager is absent for more than 2 consecutive business days, a written delegation must name a deputy and the limits delegated. Approval of deals of 500,000,000 VND or more and of Tier 3 compensation cannot be delegated below Team Leader level. Every delegation is recorded in the branch log and ends automatically when the Branch Manager returns.

## 4. Daily Operations

### 4.1 Opening Checklist [MANDATORY]

The first manager on site must complete the opening checklist by 08:45:

- Confirm that the office alarm was disarmed without incident and that the entrance access log shows no after-hours entries without approval.
- Check the customer support queue: any complaint older than 24 hours without resolution must already be at Tier 2 (DOC-07 §4.2); if not, escalate it immediately.
- Confirm that at least one Customer Support Executive is logged in for each open support channel.
- Review the day's field visits and client meetings on the branch calendar.

### 4.2 Closing Checklist [MANDATORY]

Before the office is closed at the end of the working day (DOC-01 §3.1), the last manager on site must confirm that workstations holding customer or financial data are locked, that paper documents with personal data are stored in locked cabinets (DOC-05 §3), and that the alarm is armed. Any exception is recorded in the branch log the same day.

### 4.3 Physical Security & Visitors

Visitors must sign in at reception, wear a visitor badge and be accompanied in areas where customer data is displayed. A lost access card or company laptop is a security incident and must be reported to IT Security within 1 hour of discovery (DOC-06 §5).

## 5. Weekly & Monthly Routines

### 5.1 Weekly Operations Meeting

Every Monday the Branch Manager chairs a 30-minute operations meeting with team leads. The standing agenda is: open Tier 2 and Tier 3 escalations, the sales pipeline summary submitted by Sales Executives (DOC-08 §6), staffing and leave for the coming two weeks, and partner-school visits planned by the Operations Coordinator.

### 5.2 Monthly Review

On the first business day of each month the Branch Manager reviews forecast accuracy for the previous month (DOC-08 §6), the number of escalations that breached the 48-hour Tier 2 limit (DOC-07 §4.3), expense claims above 5,000,000 VND approved during the month, and onboarding completion of new hires at the branch (DOC-11 §13).

## 6. Operations Coordinator Duties [ROLE-SPECIFIC]

The Operations Coordinator keeps the branch's delivery to partner schools running: scheduling, logistics, field visits and vendor coordination. This section is the source of the Operations-specific requirements of the Role Requirement Matrix.

### 6.1 Partner-School Onboarding [MANDATORY]

When a new partner school signs a contract, the Operations Coordinator must:

- Hold a kickoff call with the school within 3 business days of contract signature.
- Collect the school's contact and learner data only for the purpose stated in the contract, with consent recorded as required by DOC-05 §3.
- Create the school's delivery schedule in the operations calendar and share it with the Branch Manager.
- Confirm that a signed data-processing agreement exists before any learner data is exchanged with the school's systems (DOC-05 §6).

### 6.2 Field Visits [MANDATORY]

- Every field visit is planned at least 3 business days in advance on the branch calendar.
- Company systems may be accessed during field work only through the company VPN on an approved, encrypted device (DOC-06 §6).
- The office dress code does not apply while on site at a partner school, but professional behaviour standards do (DOC-04 §7 and §2).
- A field visit report must be submitted within 1 business day of the visit, listing attendees, issues raised and follow-up actions with owners.

### 6.3 Vendor & Logistics Coordination

The Operations Coordinator requests quotes for training venues, printed materials and transport, and submits purchase orders to the Branch Manager for approval within the limits of Section 3.1. Vendor invoices are forwarded to the Finance Associate for verification; expenses paid personally during a visit are claimed through DOC-09 within 15 calendar days.

### 6.4 Operational Incidents

Service disruptions at a partner school (for example the learning platform being unavailable during a scheduled session) must be logged as a customer issue and handled through the customer escalation process (DOC-07 §4). The Operations Coordinator informs the school of the expected resolution time within 2 business hours.

### 6.5 Partner-School Offboarding [MANDATORY]

When a partner-school contract ends or is terminated, the Operations Coordinator must:

- Disable the school's accounts on the learning platform on the contract end date.
- Return or delete all learner data received from the school within 30 calendar days of the contract end date, unless the contract or the law requires a longer retention period (DOC-05 §5).
- Obtain a written deletion confirmation from IT Support and file it in the branch log.
- Record the offboarding in the monthly review of Section 5.2.

## 7. Staffing & Leave Coverage

The Branch Manager approves team rotas so that every customer support channel is staffed during working hours. Leave requests follow the approval workflow of DOC-03 §6. No more than one third of a team may be on planned leave on the same day without the Branch Manager's written approval. Branch Managers are entitled to 15 days of annual leave per year (DOC-03 §2.1) and must arrange a delegation under Section 3.2 before any leave longer than 2 business days.

## 8. Business Continuity

If a site cannot open (power failure, flooding, local restrictions), the Branch Manager decides within 1 hour of the scheduled opening whether staff work remotely. Remote work during a disruption follows DOC-06 §6. Customer-facing teams at the other site take over the support queue until the affected site confirms that it is operating again, and every customer complaint received during the disruption is logged as usual.

## 9. Branch KPIs

- Share of Tier 1 customer issues acknowledged within 2 business hours (DOC-07 §4.1).
- Number of Tier 2 escalations older than 48 hours at month end.
- Field visit reports submitted within 1 business day (target: at least 95 percent).
- Partner-school kickoff calls held within 3 business days of contract signature.
- Partner-school offboardings with a filed deletion confirmation within 30 calendar days (target: 100 percent).
- New hires at the branch who completed the Week 1 onboarding stage on time (DOC-11 §6.1).
