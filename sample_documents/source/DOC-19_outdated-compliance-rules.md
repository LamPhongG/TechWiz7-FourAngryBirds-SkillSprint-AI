# Compliance & IT Rules 2021 (Outdated Compliance Rules)

> Expired on 2023-12-31. These rules were replaced by the Data Privacy Policy (DOC-05) and the Information Security Policy (DOC-06). The document is kept only to test that the system recognises outdated sources and does not teach them to employees.

## 1. Purpose

This handbook collects the compliance and IT rules that applied to all staff of the Company from 1 January 2021. At that time the Company had a single office in Ho Chi Minh City and about 120 employees, and customer data was managed in spreadsheets and a shared file server.

## 2. Legal Basis

The rules in this handbook were written to comply with Decree 52/2013 on e-commerce and the general confidentiality clauses of employment contracts. The Company had not yet adopted a dedicated personal-data protection policy.

## 3. Passwords & Accounts

### 3.1 Password Rules

Passwords must be at least 8 characters long and must be changed every 60 days. Passwords may be reused after 3 changes.

### 3.2 Two-Step Verification [OPTIONAL]

Two-step verification is recommended for email but is not required for any system.

### 3.3 Shared Accounts

Teams may share one login for the customer spreadsheet and the file server, provided the password is kept in the team's notebook in a locked drawer.

## 4. Devices & Remote Work

### 4.1 Personal Devices

Employees may use personal laptops and phones to open customer files when working from home, provided antivirus software is installed.

### 4.2 Remote Access

Remote access to the file server is available through a shared remote-desktop account. A VPN is not required.

### 4.3 USB Drives

Customer files may be copied to USB drives for meetings outside the office. The drive must be returned to the office within one week.

## 5. Customer Data Handling

### 5.1 Collection

Customer contact details may be collected at events and through the website without recording consent, as long as the data is used only by the Company.

### 5.2 Access

All staff in Sales, Customer Support and Finance may view the full customer spreadsheet, including payment information.

### 5.3 Sharing With Partners

Customer lists may be shared with partner schools and advertising agencies by email when a manager agrees verbally.

## 6. Retention

### 6.1 Employee Records

Employee records are kept for 10 years after the end of employment.

### 6.2 Customer Records

Customer records are kept indefinitely, because they may be useful for future sales.

## 7. Incidents

### 7.1 Reporting Deadline

Suspected security incidents, such as a lost laptop or a virus infection, must be reported to the IT administrator within 72 hours.

### 7.2 Customer Notification

Customers are informed of an incident only if the incident becomes public or the customer asks.

## 8. Expense Compliance

Expense claims must be submitted within 60 days with a photo of the receipt. Claims above 10,000,000 VND need the director's signature.

## 9. Approval Matrix (2021)

- Customer refunds up to 2,000,000 VND: approved by any Customer Support staff member.
- Customer refunds above 2,000,000 VND: approved by the director.
- Sales discounts: agreed by the Sales staff member with the customer, up to 20 percent, without further approval.
- Purchases for the office: approved by the office administrator up to 10,000,000 VND.
- Access to the customer spreadsheet for a new employee: granted by the team leader on the first day, without a request form.

## 10. Working Hours (2021)

Office hours were 08:00–17:00, Monday to Friday, and Saturday mornings from 08:00 to 12:00 twice a month. Probation lasted 30 days for all roles, and no structured onboarding programme existed.

## 11. Training

New employees read this handbook during their first month and sign a paper acknowledgement form kept by the office administrator. There is no online training or assessment.

## 12. Why These Rules Were Replaced

In 2025 the Company opened the Da Nang office, moved customer data to managed systems and adopted Decree 13/2023 on personal data protection. The rules in Sections 3 to 10 no longer meet the Company's legal obligations or security standards and were replaced on 2026-01-01.
